26) A website has 15 server ... how does Load balancer decide which server will get your request

      "A load balancer distributes incoming requests among multiple backend servers using algorithms 
      such as Round Robin, Least Connections, or Weighted Load Balancing. It also performs health checks
       and removes unhealthy servers from the pool. The choice depends on the application's requirements—for example,
        Round Robin for equal servers and Least Connections when request durations vary.
